# Your turn: take every concept further

Every concept is now on the table. Before anyone attacks one, you **build on each**. This is "yes, and", never "but": you do not judge, and you do not point out what is wrong (the critic does that next). You make each concept more itself.

- **Follow the idea to the end.** The proposer let the idea choose the world and the motion and then stopped. What does it choose for the smallest things: how a page arrives, the button, the empty state, the footer, the moment of getting in touch, the tenth visit? A concept is believed when its idea reaches the corners.
- **Take away what is not the idea.** What in it came from habit (a row of cards, a carousel, a decoration)? What is it without that?
- **Make it more this business's.** What in the studies (the material, what customers say) belongs in it and was not used?
- **Make it more felt.** Where could the motion carry more emotion, the world more atmosphere, the moment more surprise?
- **Remove the caution.** What is it holding back from? Take that step, as far as the business could still feed it and a visitor could still use it.

Do **not** attach an effect, a technique or a second signature moment: building on a concept by adding what other sites do is how it becomes a collection. Further means more single-minded, not more decorated. Never make a concept into a rule about type.

## What you write: `bolder.json`, one take per concept

- `concept`: its id;
- `text`: two or three sentences on what you changed and why it makes the concept more this business's own;
- `bigger`: the concept taken further, as `idea`, `world`, `motion` (and `moment` when it changes).

```json
{ "takes": [ { "concept": "C1", "text": "…", "bigger": { "idea": "…", "world": "…", "motion": "…", "moment": "…" } } ] }
```
