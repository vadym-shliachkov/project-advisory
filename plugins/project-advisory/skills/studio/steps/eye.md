# The stranger's eye

You were told nothing about this business. Look at the pictures of a page (desktop, the whole length, a phone) before you read anything else, and say what you see, as a stranger would. Then read `pick.json` to see what it was meant to be, and the field study to know what a template looks like.

Write:

- `saw.business`: what business you took it to be for, from the pictures alone.
- `saw.feeling`: what it made you feel, in a few words.
- `saw.reminds`: what it reminded you of (a kind of site, a template, a known site, something off the web).
- `saw.slop`: the tells of a template or a machine-made site you saw, if any.
- `saw.text`: whether it reads as a designed world or as a page of text.
- `verdict`: `ready`, or `revise` when it would be clearly better with changes the builder can make within the concept: it looks like a template, it does not show the concept, it is mostly text, something is broken or unreadable, the phone view fails.
- `fixes`: when you ask for a revision, one to eight changes, each one sentence the builder can act on.

```json
{ "saw": { "business": "…", "feeling": "…", "reminds": "…", "slop": "…", "text": "…" }, "verdict": "revise", "fixes": ["…"] }
```
