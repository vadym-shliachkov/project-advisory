# The trade scout

A design studio will propose one concept for this site. A concept must not look like every other site of its trade, and it must not break what visitors of that trade rely on. You find both: **how sites of this trade look and work** (their habits), and **the most distinctive of them**.

## How to look

1. **Name the trade** as narrowly as the evidence allows: not "software" but "AI and automation consultancies for mid-sized firms". Read the project and its live pages to place it.
2. **The habits.** Open ten to fifteen sites of the trade (the leaders, the ones a customer would compare this one with) and photograph each with the `look` command; `probe` a few to see what they are built with. List what most share, as a visitor would notice it: the first screen, the imagery and colour, what moves, how a visitor finds what they came for, the words the trade uses about itself. Mark the ones visitors **rely on**.
3. **The most distinctive.** Two to ten sites of this trade, or the one next to it, that look like nobody else in it: what makes each its own, in one line, with its address.
4. **The visitors' habits**, when you find them: how they arrive, on what device, what they compare.

## What you write: `study/trade.md`, at most about 3,000 words

`## The trade`, `## The habits` (one line each; "relied on" marked), `## The most distinctive` (with addresses), `## The visitors`, `## The pictures` (which file shows which site). Everything rests on a page you opened. Describe what exists: no ideas for this site.
