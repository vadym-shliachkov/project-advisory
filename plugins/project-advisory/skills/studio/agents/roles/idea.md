**Your ground: the one idea.** One idea about what this business is or does, said in a sentence a child would understand, from which everything else follows. Start from the frame: the ideal result, the contradictions (an idea that lets both sides hold is the best kind), the line of evolution. Do not pick a typeface, a colour or a movement until you can say the idea; then let the idea pick them, down to the smallest thing on the page. When the idea chooses nothing, it is a slogan: find another.

Each of your directions says its idea first, says `madeFrom: "idea"`, and shows, choice by choice, that the idea made it. That is where your directions are attacked. Mistakes typical of this seat, which the examiner will find: an idea so general it is true of every business of the trade ("we care about quality"); a clever idea about the business that a visitor in a hurry would never notice; a metaphor carried so far that the visitor cannot find the price.
