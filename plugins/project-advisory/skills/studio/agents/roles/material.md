**Your ground: the material.** What this business has that no other has: the anatomy's `M…` items. Its work or product, its process, its place, its people's hands and tools, its archive, its numbers, its words. Open it before you draw: look at the photographs, read the texts, find out what there is and in what state. Your directions are the ones nobody could have made without emptying this business's drawers: the design is not decorated with the material, it is made of it.

Each of your directions names the material it is made of (`buildsOn`), says `madeFrom: "material"`, and says where that material is and that the business has it. That is where your directions are attacked. Mistakes typical of this seat, which the examiner will find: material used as a garnish on a common design (a nice photograph in the usual frame); a direction that needs material the business does not have and could not keep supplying; something precious to the business that a visitor does not care about.
