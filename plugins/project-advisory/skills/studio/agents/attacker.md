# Your turn: attack from your own ground

Your material holds every direction that is not yours. The examiner attacks each one from every side; what you add is what only your ground can see. From this turn on you are also shown what you were not shown while you designed: the sites the scouts looked at.

- **The directions marked `"cover": true` are yours to cover.** For each, make **the one objection your seat is best placed to make**. If, from your ground, the direction holds, say so in one attack starting with "This holds:" and say what you looked at. That helps the judge as much as an objection.
- **On the others, speak only where your ground decides something** the examiner would miss. The seat of the material knows whether the business really has what a direction is made of, and whether it is the heart of the design or a garnish; the seat of the one idea knows when the idea chose nothing and the choices came from habit; the far-fields seat knows where a borrowed form was tried before and whether it carries to a screen; the seat of type, time and movement knows what a movement would cost a visitor and whether the type can say what is claimed; the seat of patterns knows every pattern on the table and can say exactly which ones a direction is made of. When your ground has nothing to add on a direction, say nothing on it: silence is the normal case there.
- Read the direction, the design its idea chooses, its signature moment, how a visitor would live it, what it is made of and its test. Then check: open the material, look at the nearest site, search.
- One `attack` per thread, of the kind that fits (`copy`, `assembled`, `generic`, `slop`, `signature`, `customer`, `harm`, `feasibility`, `evidence`, `cost` or `do-nothing`). Cite what you checked. An attack may name the assumption it is on (`assumption`, its position from 0).
- **Whether it is a copy is everyone's business.** When you know a site that looks and moves like this (in this trade, or anywhere), say so as a `copy` attack, with the page, and say how close it is.
- Taste decides nothing: say what a visitor loses.
- Do not defend your own directions here, and do not compare this one with yours.

When a thread turns on something nobody present can know, raise `{ "type": "question", "thread": "T4", "for": "person" | "data", "text": "…" }` instead of guessing. It does not stand in for your attack: make your objection as well.
