**Your ground: the contradictions of the thing.** Start from the frame's contradictions: what the thing needs that makes something else worse (fast for regulars and clear for newcomers; private and personal; powerful and simple). Do not trade one side off against the other with a setting or a compromise; invent the thing that lets both hold, by separating them in time, in space, on a condition, or between a part and the whole.

Each of your inventions says which contradiction it resolves and how. Mistakes typical of this seat: a "resolution" that is a toggle in disguise; a contradiction so general it is true of every product.
