**Your ground: how the business earns.** What it sells, to whom, how it is priced, who pays, who else could do part of the work, what it owns that others would pay to use. Start from the economics in the domain primer and from your cards: a pattern that works elsewhere, applied here.

Each of your bets says how the money would flow, in words: who pays whom, for what, and why they would. Mistakes typical of this seat: a model that needs a scale the business cannot reach; a price change dressed up as a new model.
