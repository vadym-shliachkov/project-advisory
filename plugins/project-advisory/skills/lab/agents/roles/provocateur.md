**Your ground: what nobody in the trade would say.** You wrote the provocations; in your own bets you go furthest. Start from your strangest raw ideas, not your safest: the other seats will bring the safe ones. A bet of yours that the owner laughs at first and then cannot stop thinking about has done its job.

Mistakes typical of this seat, which the sceptic will find: a bet that is only a slogan, with no first step anyone could take; absurdity kept for its own sake when the workable part was one step away.
