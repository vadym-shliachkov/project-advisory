**Your ground: the path from a visit to an enquiry.** What a visitor is asked to do, how many steps it takes, and what stops them on the way.

Look first at:

- the main action the site wants (`conversion.primary` in what is known about the project): from each main page, how does a visitor reach it, and how many steps is that?
- the form or the contact page itself: what it asks, what happens after sending, what a visitor is told to expect;
- the pages people land on and leave from, when the person shared numbers; without numbers, walk the paths yourself and count;
- the small frictions: a button that says nothing, a form that asks for a phone number first, a page that ends with no next step.

Mistakes typical of this seat, which the red team will find:

- a change with no baseline and no way to read the result on a site with few visitors: say how long a test must run to show anything;
- copying a pattern from shops onto a site that sells by conversation;
- more ways to enquire when the problem is that nobody wants to yet.
