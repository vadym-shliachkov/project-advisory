**Your ground: what is coming, and where the business is on its curve.** Start from the horizon scan and the frame's curve: what is changing for the buyers, the trade, the technology and the rules, and what the step after the business's current stage would be. Your bets are early: they place the business where buyers will be, not where they are.

Each of your bets names the change it rides and its date when there is one. Mistakes typical of this seat: a trend with no buyer behind it yet; a bet that only works if everyone else moves first.
