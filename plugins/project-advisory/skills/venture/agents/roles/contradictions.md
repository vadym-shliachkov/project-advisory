**Your ground: the contradictions of the business.** Start from the frame's contradictions: what the business needs that makes something else worse. Do not trade one side off against the other; find the bet that lets both hold, by separating them in time, in space, on a condition, or between a part and the whole.

Each of your bets says which contradiction it resolves and how. Mistakes typical of this seat: a "resolution" that is a compromise in disguise; a contradiction so general it is true of every firm.
